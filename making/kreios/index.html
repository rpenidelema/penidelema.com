Test hardware for plasma thrusters

I spent the summer of 2026 inside a vacuum-chamber test facility, designing and machining what it was missing.

  • Kreios SpaceAssembly, integration and test intern, summer 2026
  • Three noble gasesThrough a flow-control panel I designed and machined
  • Three sensing systemsMounted inside the chamber on supports I built
  • Mostly wordsThe work is confidential under a NATO agreement

The work

I built a control panel for the flow of three noble gases, and supports for three sensing systems inside the chamber. Between builds I ran impedance tests and diagnosed plasma across thrust setups.

I also started documenting the tests that, until then, had gone unrecorded.

Much of this work is confidential, so this page is told mostly in words, and the room is all you see.
