Sensing an electron beam

At NorthStar I am helping design the system that senses the beam of an electron accelerator.

  • NorthStar2026 to now
  • An electron beamInside a particle accelerator
  • Simulated firstModelled in FLUKA before it is built

The work

We are designing a sensing system that works inside the accelerator itself, where the beam runs. Before anything is built, we model how it will behave in FLUKA, a code for simulating particles passing through matter.

The project is under way; more here as it can be shared.
