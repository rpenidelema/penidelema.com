Sensing an electron beam

NorthStar, 2026 to now

Inside a particle accelerator

Modelled in FLUKA before it is built

At NorthStar I am helping design the system that senses the beam of an electron accelerator.

It works inside the accelerator itself, where the beam runs. Before anything is built, we model how it will behave in FLUKA, a code for simulating particles passing through matter.

The project is under way; more here as it can be shared.
